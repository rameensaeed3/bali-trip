Thu · May 7
Day 1
Tegallalang · Elephant Safari Park · Ubud Market & Palace · Campuhan Sunset · Arabian Knight
Ubud · Scooter
Puri Andong Villa
9:00 AM
Rent scooter from Puri Andong or nearby shop
Ask hotel reception or walk to any rental shop on Jl. Raya Ubud — IDR 70,000–100,000/day (~$4–6). Bring California motorcycle license and cash. Avoid leaving passport as deposit — offer IDR 200,000–300,000 cash deposit instead. ⚠️ Photograph the scooter all around before riding off to document pre-existing damage. Download Google Maps offline for Ubud/Gianyar/Bangli region the night before.
9:30 AM
Tegallalang Rice Terraces
~20 mins north on the scooter — scenic ride through Ubud outskirts and village roads. Entry IDR 15,000. Find a clifftop café for coffee over the terraces. Skip the tourist swings. ~45–60 mins. Easy parking at the terraces.
10:45 AM
Mason Elephant Safari Park, Taro Manaal
Confirmed booking ✅ · General Admission · 2 adults · $44.50. ~10 mins from Tegallalang. Easy scooter parking at the entrance. Hand-feed and interact with rescued Sumatran elephants. Allow ~2 hrs — feeding zone, treetop walk, museum, elephant show. Note: elephant riding discontinued as of Jan 2026.
1:00 PM
Lunch at Huma Cafe Manaal
Ride back south into Ubud — natural lunch stop on the way in from Taro. Beautiful garden setting, good coffee and food. Confirm pork-free when ordering. ~1 hr. Scooter parking easy outside.
2:30 PM
Back to Puri Andong Villa — freshen up
Quick shower and change before heading back out. Keep the scooter for the afternoon and evening.
3:15 PM
Ubud Market & Ubud Palace Manaal
~5 min ride from Puri Andong. Scooter parking near the market on Jl. Raya Ubud. Ubud Market: batik, silver, spices, souvenirs — bargain hard, opening prices are 2–3×. Ubud Palace (Puri Saren) is right next door and free to enter. ~1 hr total.
4:30 PM
Campuhan Ridge Walk at Sunset
~5 min ride west along Jl. Raya Campuhan. Small parking area right at the trailhead. Free, open 24 hrs. 2–3 km ridgeline walk through green Ubud hills. Arrive by 5:00 PM for golden hour. Bring water. Leave the scooter at the trailhead while you walk.
7:00 PM
Dinner at Arabian Knight Ubud
🕌 Best halal-certified restaurant in Ubud — lamb mandi, kabsa, shawarma. On-site musholla. Open until 1 AM. Jl. Suweta No.1. ~5 min ride from Campuhan. Return scooter to hotel or rental shop after dinner.

Sacred spring purification temple — one of Bali's most spiritually significant sites. Dress modestly, sarong required (available at gate). Entry IDR 50,000. ~45 mins.

~8 hr total tour duration per booking. Drop-off back at hotel. Shower, change, and rest before dinner.

Sat · May 9
Day 3
⭐ Mt. Batur Sunrise · Kintamani Viewpoint · Hot Springs · Rest
North Bali / Ubud · Guided Tour
Puri Andong Villa
2:30 AM
Mt Batur Jeep + Trekking pickup from Puri Andong Villa
Confirmed booking ✅ · Private Sunrise Trekking + Hot Spring · $99.80 for 2 · Pickup from Puri Andong Villa. Driver will contact via WhatsApp the afternoon before to confirm exact time. Wear warm layers — cold at the summit. Bring headlamp.
4:30 AM
Summit climb — 2 hrs
Steep volcanic path. Guide provides flashlight and a breakfast cooked in volcanic steam at the top — eggs and toast, one of Indonesia's most memorable small moments.
6:00 AM
Sunrise from the crater rim
Views of Mt. Agung, the caldera lake, and the Bali coast on clear days. One of the most iconic experiences in Indonesia.
8:30 AM
Kintamani / Penelokan caldera rim viewpoint
On the descent road — stop for coffee and photos along the clifftop ridge overlooking Lake Batur and the volcano you just climbed. Spectacular perspective from up here. ~20–30 mins. Skip the food at the tourist restaurants — overpriced and mediocre.
10:00 AM
Toya Devasya Natural Hot Springs — soak + brunch
~20 mins descent from the rim. Soak first in the volcanic pools overlooking the caldera lake, then have a proper late breakfast/brunch at their halal-friendly restaurant once you're clean and relaxed. Entry + pool ~IDR 250,000/person. Much better food here than anywhere on the rim.
12:30 PM
Return to Ubud
~1.5 hrs drive. You've been up since 2:30 AM — no agenda for the afternoon.
Afternoon
Rest, nap, or wander
A slow warung lunch, another massage if you feel like it, or simply sleep. Tomorrow is a dedicated Sekumpul day — rest up.
Evening
Light dinner, early night
Warung Wardani or Arabian Knight for a simple halal dinner. Early to bed — tomorrow is a dedicated Sekumpul day.
Sun · May 10
Day 4
Sekumpul Waterfall · Ulun Danu Beratan Temple · Jatiluwih Rice Terraces
North Bali · Private Driver
Puri Andong Villa
8:00 AM
Depart Puri Andong → Sekumpul, North Bali
Confirmed private car charter ✅ · 12 hrs · $35.40. ~1.5–2 hrs from Ubud. Early start is key — arrive before midday heat and tour group crowds. Bring non-slip shoes, dry bag, change of clothes, and cash for the local guide.
9:45 AM
Sekumpul Waterfall
Hire a local guide at the entrance (~IDR 100,000–150,000) — essential as the path crosses private land and splits across multiple falls. Entry IDR 20,000. Steep descent of ~300 steps into the jungle ravine. Allow 2.5–3 hrs to explore fully. Warung at the entrance for a light lunch or snack after.
1:00 PM
Lunch near Sekumpul entrance
Local warung at the trailhead — simple Indonesian food, very cheap. Rest and refuel before the drive south.
2:00 PM
Ulun Danu Beratan Temple
~40 mins south of Sekumpul. Iconic floating Hindu temple on the misty volcanic crater lake of Beratan — one of Bali's most photographed sites. Entry IDR 75,000. Sarong required (available to borrow at gate). ~45 mins. The afternoon mist over the lake makes this time of day particularly atmospheric.
3:15 PM
Jatiluwih Rice Terraces
~45 mins from Ulun Danu. UNESCO World Heritage-listed — far more expansive and less touristy than Tegallalang. Rolling terraced hillsides stretching to the horizon. Entry IDR 50,000. A short walk along the terrace path (~30–45 mins) is enough to take it all in. The late afternoon light here is beautiful.
5:30 PM
Drive back to Ubud
~1.5 hrs from Jatiluwih. Arrive hotel ~7:00 PM. Light dinner at Warung Wardani or Arabian Knight. Early night — checking out tomorrow morning for Seminyak.

Surf lesson on Seminyak Beach
Beginner-friendly — many instructors set up directly on the beach, no booking needed. ~1.5 hrs, ~$25–35/person. Seminyak has gentler waves than Canggu or Uluwatu, ideal for first-timers. Bring a change of clothes.

Independent fashion, jewelry, and homewares on Jl. Kayu Aya and Jl. Laksmana. Low-pressure, good for an hour of browsing before lunch.

Lunch or High Tea — Cafe Organic / Neon Palms or Biku Bali Manaal
Cafe Organic and Neon Palms are halal-friendly lunch options. Biku Bali is a beautiful 150-yr-old teak joglo for high tea (IDR 160,000 for two) — ⚠️ not halal-certified, serves alcohol, pork-free menu. Pick one based on your mood.

Pre-arranged driver departs for Uluwatu
Confirmed private car charter ✅ · 6 hrs · $27.41. ~1 hr 20 mins from Seminyak. Do NOT rely on Grab for the return — hundreds of tourists leave simultaneously after the Kecak dance. Entry IDR 50,000. Sarong required. ⚠️ Monkeys steal glasses and loose items — secure everything before entering.
5:30 PM
Arrive Uluwatu — explore temple grounds
~30 mins before the Kecak show to walk the clifftop, watch the monkeys, and take in the ocean views before finding your seat.
6:00 PM
Kecak Fire Dance at sunset Manaal
Confirmed booking ✅ · 2 adults · $17.80 · 18:00 show. Open-air clifftop stage over the Indian Ocean. 50+ chanting men, Ramayana story, sun sets behind them. Bali's most spectacular performance.
7:30 PM
Jimbaran Bay beachside seafood
30 mins from Uluwatu. Grilled fresh catch on the beach. Nyoman Cafe is Muslim-friendly. Return to Seminyak — pack tonight, early fast boat tomorrow.

Two connected falls — Sendang Gile (lower, easy) and Tiu Kelep (upper, 30-min jungle trek with river crossings). Entry IDR 20,000. Traditional Sasak indigenous village, 20 mins scooter from Kuta. Ende is smaller and more authentic; Sade is larger and more tourism-organized. Thatched houses, traditional weaving. Entry + guide IDR 20,000–50,000. ~1 hr.

Ride the coastal road east and west of Kuta at your own pace. Views of the bays, quiet roads, local life. No agenda — just enjoy being on two wheels in Lombok.

Find a local mosque near Kuta Lombok for Friday prayers — several within easy scooter distance. Dress modestly, remove shoes. One of the most immersive experiences you can have in Lombok — the call to prayer carries across the whole town and the community atmosphere is genuinely special. ~45 mins including prayer.

Halal lunch in Kuta Lombok
Nasi Balap Puyung — local Lombok rice dish with spiced shredded chicken, fried tempe, sambal. IDR 20,000–30,000 at any warung. Also the last chance to try Ayam Taliwang.
3:00 PM

10 mins east of Kuta on scooter. Two coves with different sand textures side by side — one white, one pepper-like. Beautiful and uncrowded.
5:30 PM
Bukit Merese Hill at Sunset
15-min easy hike, 360° panoramic views — Indian Ocean one side, Kuta Bay the other. Free entry. Just 10 mins from Kuta on the scooter. One of Lombok's most beautiful sunset spots.

Final Lombok dinner · pack for tomorrow
Book your midday fast boat back to Bali tonight if not already done. No early start needed tomorrow — Day 10 begins with a relaxed morning drive northwest toward Mataram.

2 — Practical Tips

Money & ATMs

Withdraw IDR from BNI, BCA, or Mandiri ATMs (~$170–200 daily limit). Carry cash always — cards not accepted at most Gili and Lombok spots.

SIM Card

Buy Telkomsel at DPS arrivals — best coverage across all three islands. 10GB for ~IDR 75,000 (~$5). Passport required. Activate before leaving the airport. eSIM via Airalo also works if you prefer not to swap physical cards.

Tipping

Drivers: IDR 50,000–150,000/day depending on length. Massage therapists: IDR 20,000–50,000. Guides (Batur, Sekumpul, Tiu Kelep): IDR 50,000–100,000.

3 — Halal Food Guide

Items marked 🕌 are MUI-certified or explicitly halal-certified. Items marked ⚠️ are pork-free but not formally certified — always confirm with staff when ordering.

Ubud

Arabian Knight Ubud 🕌 MUI Certified · Musholla On-Site
Best halal restaurant in Ubud. Lamb mandi, kabsa, shawarma, hummus, MANDI rice. On-site prayer room. Open 9 AM–1 AM. Jl. Suweta No.1, near Ubud Palace. Widely reviewed by Muslim travelers as the go-to.
Warung Wardani 🕌 Halal Certified
Traditional Indonesian warung on Jl. Gootama. Nasi Campur, Gado-Gado, Lawar. IDR 40,000–70,000/meal. Beloved by locals — no tourist markup.
Bale Udang Mang Engking 🕌 Halal Certified
Unique experience — dining in floating bamboo gazebos over a fish pond. Famous for honey-grilled prawns. Special atmosphere for a nice dinner. Multiple Bali locations.
Seniman Coffee ⚠️ Pork-Free — Not MUI Certified Manaal
Ubud's best specialty coffee. Indonesian and Western breakfast. No pork. Jl. Sriwedari No.5. Opens 8 AM.
Huma Cafe ⚠️ Pork-Free — Confirm When Ordering Manaal
Newer Ubud café with beautiful setting. Good for breakfast or lunch. Confirm halal prep with staff.

Seminyak

Warung Sunset 🕌 MUI Certified
Jl. Petitenget. Nasi Goreng, Mie Goreng, Indonesian staples. IDR 30,000–80,000. Open until 11 PM. Reliable certified pick close to all recommended hotels.
Queen's Tandoor Seminyak 🕌 Halal Certified
Indian halal — curries, biryani, naan, tandoori chicken. Upscale pricing (~IDR 100,000–200,000/dish). Multiple Bali locations. Worth it for a special dinner.
Cafe Organic Petitenget ⚠️ Halal-Friendly Manaal
Healthy, clean eating, beautiful garden setting. Good for light lunch or breakfast. Jl. Petitenget area.
Bias Courtyard / Sea Circus / Bo n Bun / Neon Palms ⚠️ Pork-Free — Confirm Halal Manaal
All on or near Jl. Petitenget. Pork-free options at each — confirm halal prep when ordering. Good for café crawl on Day 4 morning.
Biku Bali ⚠️ Not Halal Certified — Serves Alcohol Manaal
Beautiful 150-yr-old teak joglo. Pork-free menu, Indonesian high tea IDR 160,000 for two. Serves alcohol — visit with awareness if you choose to go. Stick to Indonesian dishes and tea.

Lombok — Halal by Default Everywhere

All Local Warungs 🕌 Halal by Default
Lombok is 90% Muslim. Every local warung is halal. No pork on menus anywhere. The most stress-free eating zone of the entire trip — no checking, no asking, no concerns.
🍗 Must Try: Ayam Taliwang
Lombok's signature dish — grilled chicken (whole or half) with a fiery chili-tomato sauce. Order with Plecing Kangkung (water spinach with sambal). Every warung in Kuta Lombok serves it. IDR 40,000–80,000.
🍚 Must Try: Nasi Balap Puyung
Local Lombok rice dish — shredded spiced chicken, fried tempe, sambal, bean sprouts. Quick, cheap, and delicious for breakfast or lunch. IDR 20,000–30,000.
Hubbul Watan Grand Mosque Canteen, Mataram Manaal
One of Lombok's most historic mosques — worth visiting the grounds. Simple halal canteen food nearby if you pass through Mataram on Day 8 or 9.
