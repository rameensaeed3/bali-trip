2 — Tips & Recommendations

Trusted Driver — Bali

Made Arta · WhatsApp: +62 878 6150 5339
Reliable, English-speaking. Great for day trips and transfers.

Lombok Drivers & Fast Boats

Travel Time Lombok · WhatsApp: +62 877-9278-6000
Drivers + fast boat bookings across Lombok and the Gilis.

Money & ATMs

Withdraw IDR from BNI, BCA, or Mandiri ATMs (~$170–200 daily limit). Withdraw large amounts at once — each transaction has a fixed fee.

SIM Card

Buy Telkomsel at DPS arrivals. 10GB ~IDR 75,000 (~$5), passport required. eSIM also available. Activate before leaving the airport.

Tipping

Drivers: IDR 50,000–150,000/day depending on length. Guides: IDR 50,000–100,000.

Shopping — Prioritize Ubud

Ubud is the cheapest for souvenirs. Seminyak is overpriced. Bargain hard — opening prices are 2–3× fair value.

Preferred Route

Better route: Ubud → Gili T (via Padangbai) → Lombok → Seminyak. Less hotel switching, spend 2–3 nights on Gili T for a proper island feel, and end in Seminyak before flying home.

What to Pack

Sunscreen SPF 50+, a hat, and loose clothing (linen/dresses) — jeans are too hot. Universal charging adaptor (Type C/F).

Lombok Rec

Tiu Kelep Waterfall, Senaru — two connected falls, river crossing to reach the upper one. Entry IDR 20,000, local guide ~IDR 50,000. Bring water shoes and a change of clothes.

Booking Activities

Book tours and activities on GetYourGuide or Klook — free cancellation options, verified reviews, and competitive pricing.

3 — Food Guide

Items marked 🕌 are MUI-certified or explicitly halal-certified. Items marked ⚠️ are pork-free but not formally certified — always confirm with staff when ordering.

Ubud

Arabian Knight Ubud 🕌 MUI Certified · Musholla On-Site
Best halal restaurant in Ubud. Lamb mandi, kabsa, shawarma, hummus, MANDI rice. On-site prayer room. Open 9 AM–1 AM. Jl. Suweta No.1, near Ubud Palace. Widely reviewed by Muslim travelers as the go-to.
Antawali Kitchen 🕌 Halal
Local Balinese kitchen with fresh ingredients and a relaxed atmosphere. A great dinner spot in Ubud.
Jabarano Coffee 🕌 Halal
Cozy café — good for a relaxed dinner or evening wind-down after a big day. Great coffee and light bites.
Acai Queen
Acai bowls and desserts. Perfect post-dinner treat in Ubud.
Tukies Coconut Shop
Famous for coconut-based desserts and ice cream. A Ubud institution — don't leave without trying it.
Huma Cafe ⚠️ Pork-Free — Confirm When Ordering
Beautiful garden setting. Good for lunch. Natural stop on the Taro/Tegallalang route. Confirm halal prep with staff.
Seniman Coffee ⚠️ Pork-Free — Not MUI Certified
Ubud's best specialty coffee. Indonesian and Western breakfast. No pork. Jl. Sriwedari No.5. Opens 8 AM.

Seminyak

Sumak Turkish Cuisine 🕌 Halal
Halal Turkish restaurant in Seminyak. Kebabs, grills, and Turkish classics.
Cafe Organic Petitenget ⚠️ Halal-Friendly
Healthy, clean eating, beautiful garden setting. Good for light lunch or breakfast. Jl. Petitenget area.
Bias Courtyard / Sea Circus / Bo n Bun / Neon Palms ⚠️ Pork-Free — Confirm Halal
All on or near Jl. Petitenget. Pork-free options at each — confirm halal prep when ordering.
Biku Bali ⚠️ Not Halal Certified — Serves Alcohol
Beautiful 150-yr-old teak joglo. Pork-free menu, Indonesian high tea IDR 160,000 for two. Serves alcohol — visit with awareness if you choose to go.

Gili Islands

Istanbul Kebab 2 🕌 Halal
Solid halal Turkish kebabs on Gili T. Easy to find on the main strip.
Muslim-owned warungs along the main strip
Look for "Halal" signs. Fresh grilled fish, nasi goreng, Indonesian staples. Common throughout Gili T.

Lombok — Halal by Default Everywhere

All Local Warungs 🕌 Halal by Default
Lombok is 90% Muslim. Every local warung is halal. No pork on menus anywhere. The most stress-free eating zone of the entire trip — no checking, no asking, no concerns.
