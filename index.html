Sat · May 9
Day 3
North Bali / Ubud · Guided Tour
Puri Andong Villa
⭐ Mt. Batur Sunrise · Kintamani Viewpoint · Hot Springs · Rest
2:30 AM
Mt Batur Jeep + Trekking pickup from Puri Andong Villa
Confirmed ✅ · $99.80 · Pickup from Puri Andong. Wear warm layers, bring headlamp. Driver WhatsApps the afternoon before.
4:30 AM
Summit climb — 2 hrs
Steep volcanic path. Guide provides flashlight and a breakfast cooked in volcanic steam at the top — eggs and toast, one of Indonesia's most memorable small moments.
6:00 AM
Sunrise from the crater rim
Views of Mt. Agung, the caldera lake, and the Bali coast on clear days. One of the most iconic experiences in Indonesia.
8:30 AM
Kintamani / Penelokan caldera rim viewpoint
On the descent road — stop for coffee and photos along the clifftop ridge overlooking Lake Batur and the volcano you just climbed. Spectacular perspective from up here. ~20–30 mins. Skip the food at the tourist restaurants — overpriced and mediocre.
10:00 AM
Toya Devasya Natural Hot Springs — soak + brunch
~20 mins descent from the rim. Soak first in the volcanic pools overlooking the caldera lake, then have a proper late breakfast/brunch at their halal-friendly restaurant once you're clean and relaxed. Entry + pool ~IDR 250,000/person. Much better food here than anywhere on the rim.
12:30 PM
Return to Ubud
~1.5 hrs drive. You've been up since 2:30 AM — no agenda for the afternoon.
Afternoon
Rest, nap, or wander
A slow warung lunch, another massage if you feel like it, or simply sleep. Tomorrow is a dedicated Sekumpul day — rest up.
Evening
Light dinner, early night
Warung Wardani or Arabian Knight for a simple halal dinner. Early to bed — tomorrow is a dedicated Sekumpul day.

2 — Practical Tips

Money & ATMs

Withdraw IDR from BNI, BCA, or Mandiri ATMs (~$170–200 daily limit). Carry cash always — cards not accepted at most Gili and Lombok spots.

SIM Card

Buy Telkomsel at DPS arrivals — best coverage across all three islands. 10GB for ~IDR 75,000 (~$5). Passport required. Activate before leaving the airport. eSIM via Airalo also works if you prefer not to swap physical cards.

Tipping

Drivers: IDR 50,000–150,000/day depending on length. Massage therapists: IDR 20,000–50,000. Guides (Batur, Sekumpul, Tiu Kelep): IDR 50,000–100,000.

3 — Halal Food Guide

Items marked 🕌 are MUI-certified or explicitly halal-certified. Items marked ⚠️ are pork-free but not formally certified — always confirm with staff when ordering.

Ubud

Arabian Knight Ubud 🕌 MUI Certified · Musholla On-Site
Best halal restaurant in Ubud. Lamb mandi, kabsa, shawarma, hummus, MANDI rice. On-site prayer room. Open 9 AM–1 AM. Jl. Suweta No.1, near Ubud Palace. Widely reviewed by Muslim travelers as the go-to.
Warung Wardani 🕌 Halal Certified
Traditional Indonesian warung on Jl. Gootama. Nasi Campur, Gado-Gado, Lawar. IDR 40,000–70,000/meal. Beloved by locals — no tourist markup.
Bale Udang Mang Engking 🕌 Halal Certified
Unique experience — dining in floating bamboo gazebos over a fish pond. Famous for honey-grilled prawns. Special atmosphere for a nice dinner. Multiple Bali locations.
Seniman Coffee ⚠️ Pork-Free — Not MUI Certified Manaal
Ubud's best specialty coffee. Indonesian and Western breakfast. No pork. Jl. Sriwedari No.5. Opens 8 AM.
Huma Cafe ⚠️ Pork-Free — Confirm When Ordering Manaal
Newer Ubud café with beautiful setting. Good for breakfast or lunch. Confirm halal prep with staff.

Seminyak

Warung Sunset 🕌 MUI Certified
Jl. Petitenget. Nasi Goreng, Mie Goreng, Indonesian staples. IDR 30,000–80,000. Open until 11 PM. Reliable certified pick close to all recommended hotels.
Queen's Tandoor Seminyak 🕌 Halal Certified
Indian halal — curries, biryani, naan, tandoori chicken. Upscale pricing (~IDR 100,000–200,000/dish). Multiple Bali locations. Worth it for a special dinner.
Cafe Organic Petitenget ⚠️ Halal-Friendly Manaal
Healthy, clean eating, beautiful garden setting. Good for light lunch or breakfast. Jl. Petitenget area.
Bias Courtyard / Sea Circus / Bo n Bun / Neon Palms ⚠️ Pork-Free — Confirm Halal Manaal
All on or near Jl. Petitenget. Pork-free options at each — confirm halal prep when ordering. Good for café crawl on Day 4 morning.
Biku Bali ⚠️ Not Halal Certified — Serves Alcohol Manaal
Beautiful 150-yr-old teak joglo. Pork-free menu, Indonesian high tea IDR 160,000 for two. Serves alcohol — visit with awareness if you choose to go. Stick to Indonesian dishes and tea.

Lombok — Halal by Default Everywhere

All Local Warungs 🕌 Halal by Default
Lombok is 90% Muslim. Every local warung is halal. No pork on menus anywhere. The most stress-free eating zone of the entire trip — no checking, no asking, no concerns.
🍗 Must Try: Ayam Taliwang
Lombok's signature dish — grilled chicken (whole or half) with a fiery chili-tomato sauce. Order with Plecing Kangkung (water spinach with sambal). Every warung in Kuta Lombok serves it. IDR 40,000–80,000.
🍚 Must Try: Nasi Balap Puyung
Local Lombok rice dish — shredded spiced chicken, fried tempe, sambal, bean sprouts. Quick, cheap, and delicious for breakfast or lunch. IDR 20,000–30,000.
Hubbul Watan Grand Mosque Canteen, Mataram Manaal
One of Lombok's most historic mosques — worth visiting the grounds. Simple halal canteen food nearby if you pass through Mataram on Day 8 or 9.
